Drift — Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Drift records which websites you visit and how long you spend on each page. This data includes URLs, page titles, timestamps, and duration. No page content, form inputs, passwords, or personal information is collected.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using the Chrome storage API. Your browsing data never leaves your computer. There are no external servers, no cloud storage, and no syncing.

Data Sharing

Drift does not transmit any data to any third party. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels, and no advertising. Your data stays on your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time from the Drift dashboard. Uninstalling the extension also removes all data.

Permissions

tabs — Required to detect which tab is active and read page URLs for session tracking.

activeTab — Required to access the current tab's information.

storage — Required to save browsing events locally on your device.

idle — Required to detect when you step away, so sessions are properly ended.

No Account Required

Drift does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project repository.
